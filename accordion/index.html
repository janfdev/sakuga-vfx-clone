Accordion

Collapsible panels that expand with dust and ズザッ.

Sakuga refers to sequences in anime where the quality and fluidity of animation spikes dramatically. Key animators sell momentum through extreme squash, overshoot, and expressive hand-drawn visual effects.
Each Sakuga control is completely self-contained in a single file. No npm modules, no framework runtime, and no build configuration necessary.
Instead of pre-baked cubic-bezier easing curves, motions are calculated using spring physics with configurable stiffness and damping.

Click or press Enter/Space on any header to expand or collapse.
